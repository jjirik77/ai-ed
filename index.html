    Faculty AI Integration Studio

    A research-based learning studio for faculty in every discipline. You bring one real course. Over eight weeks you test AI against your own teaching problems, validate what it produces, and leave with a pilot-ready plan, or a reasoned decision not to use AI.

    Why this studio

    Faculty need more than tool demonstrations

    In a 2025 survey of 1,681 faculty in 28 countries, 40% said they were just beginning their AI-literacy journey, 80% reported a lack of clarity on how AI can be applied in teaching at their institution, and only 6% fully agreed that their institution had provided enough resources to build faculty AI literacy (Digital Education Council, 2025). This studio responds with practice on real courses, peer critique, and a clear standard for what "ready to pilot" means.

    Capability model

    Eight things you will be able to do

    The model aligns with the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024) and the EDUCAUSE AI Literacy in Teaching and Learning faculty outcomes (2024). Each capability has evidence in the portfolio.

    Conceptual literacy

    Explain AI, LLMs, training, inference, tokens, and limits in plain language.

    Evidence: AI mental model

    Intentional use

    Decide whether AI fits an educational problem before choosing a tool.

    Evidence: Opportunity-risk map

    Tool judgment

    Compare tools on pedagogy, accuracy, privacy, accessibility, equity, cost, and control.

    Evidence: Tool scorecard

    Prompt and workflow design

    Specify goals, context, constraints, format, criteria, and a review loop.

    Evidence: Tested workflow

    Validation

    Verify claims, citations, data, calculations, bias, and completeness.

    Evidence: Annotated audit

    Pedagogical integration

    Use AI to support outcomes, active learning, feedback, and metacognition.

    Evidence: Accessible artifact + activity

    Assessment stewardship

    Redesign assessments, clarify AI use, and keep final grading human.

    Evidence: Assessment + policy

    Ethical agency

    Protect privacy, IP, access, autonomy, and fairness; document decisions.

    Evidence: Ethics record + pilot

    How each week works

    A predictable studio rhythm

    MinutesMovePurpose
    0–5Check-inRetrieval prompt, poll, or "what changed in practice?"
    5–15ConceptOnly the theory needed for the day's decisions, with one contrasting example
    15–25Think-aloudMake expert judgment and validation visible
    25–43Hands-onApply the concept to your own course
    43–53Peer critiqueImprove the artifact with a cross-disciplinary peer
    53–58CommitmentTurn feedback into a next step
    58–60Exit ticketCheck understanding and name support needs

    Fieldwork follows a 10–40–10 pattern: 10 minutes reviewing a short resource, 40 minutes applying the method to a real course artifact, and 10 minutes posting it and giving one peer useful feedback.
