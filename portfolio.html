    AI Integration Portfolio

    The standardCompletion means implementation readiness, not advocacy for AI. A well-supported decision to keep a non-AI workflow can meet the standard.

    Artifacts: 8Built: a teachable unitRubric: 6 criteria × 4 levelsSaves: this browser onlyExport: Word, PDF, backup file

    How it works

    One course, eight artifacts, one plan

    Each module adds one artifact. Four of them also record your course build deliverables from the Course Build Studio: the repository, the unit framework, the lessons, and the activities. Fill in the template after the module's fieldwork. Entries save automatically in this browser as you type. They are not sent anywhere. Select Save progress under any artifact to keep everything in one file you can open on any computer.

    Responsible use

    Do not enter identifiable student information, grades, or confidential records here. Describe students in general terms ("a multilingual first-year student"), and use fictional or de-identified examples.

    Rubric self-check

    Is your portfolio pilot ready?

    Rate your whole portfolio on each criterion. Pilot ready on all six is the completion standard. Your facilitator and a peer use the same rubric.

    Choose a level for each criterion.
